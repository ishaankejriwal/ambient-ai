Can Epic's EHI export score the clinical decision support metrics?

The framework spreadsheet lists 15 methods and metrics for AI clinical decision support (CDS). This page writes each one as a formula in plain words, then shows which Epic EHI export table and column supplies each piece of it. Pieces Epic does not have are marked in red, with where to get them.

Of the 15 metrics, . Partly means Epic has some of the inputs, such as the patient groups or the records a reviewer reads, and a reviewer, a survey or the vendor supplies the rest.

The same 15 cards exist for Oracle Health and athenahealth. The formula and the variables are the same on all three pages, and only the tables and columns change.

Table, row and column

Table
One file in the export, like one tab of a spreadsheet. AI_INTRCT_INFO is the table of Epic AI sessions.
Row
One line in that table. In AI_INTRCT_INFO one row is one Epic AI session. In PATIENT one row is one patient.
Column
One fact that every row has. AI_INTRCT_AGENT_RESPONSES.AGENT_RESPONSE holds the answer the AI returned.

Most formulas count rows, so each card says what one row stands for in every table it uses, and each chip in a formula repeats it.

The box around a variable shows where it stands. Visits is in the export. Note time is partly there: the export has a stand-in or part of the answer, and the rest comes from somewhere else. Survey score is not in the export.

The status beside each metric's name follows from those boxes. Yes means every variable is in the export. Partly means some are, or the export has a stand-in. No means none are. Some cards end the In Epic part with "Also in the export". Those are columns that help but sit outside the formula, and they do not change the status.

Every rate and percent change comes out as a fraction. 0.05 is 5 percent, or 5 percentage points when it is the gap between two rates. Count each case once. If the bottom of a fraction is zero, report N/A.

How to read the Epic site

The Epic EHI table index lists the export schema. Each table page describes its primary key and columns. Run calculations on your organization's exported files after checking its Epic version and local configuration.

  • Columns ending in _C_NAME are pick-lists. The page lists the allowed values, and sites can add their own.
  • Most provider, payer, procedure and diagnosis IDs come out as names. You get VISIT_PROV_ID_PROV_NAME, not VISIT_PROV_ID. Patient, visit, note, account and user IDs are real IDs.
  • DATETIME (UTC) and DATETIME (Local) are real timestamps. For plain DATETIME, check the column description before assuming it contains a time of day.
  • Tables join on PAT_ENC_CSN_ID (visit), NOTE_ID (note), PAT_ID (patient), USER_ID (the person logged in) and TX_ID (a charge or payment).

The tables that matter here

  • AI_INTRCT_INFO and the other AI tables: Epic's own AI sessions, with the user, the patient, the request, the answer and the status history.
  • PATIENT, PATIENT_RACE, PATIENT_4 and PAT_ACCT_CVG: the traits that make up the patient groups on the fairness cards.
  • ORDER_MED, ALT_ORDINFO and ALT_HISTORY: medication orders, and the medication warnings Epic fired on them.
  • NOTE_EDIT_TRAIL and HNO_PLAIN_TEXT: note editing times and note text, for the workload cards.

Where the AI shows up

  • Epic's own AI writes one row per session to AI_INTRCT_INFO. AI_INTRCT_CONTACT_INFO.AI_INTRCT_SESS_TYPE_C_NAME says which workflow it helped with. Ask your Epic analyst which value is your decision support tool.
  • A vendor tool that runs outside Epic leaves nothing in these tables. Ask the vendor for its log of requests, answers and times, with patient and visit IDs.
  • The developer runs many of these metrics on test cases before go-live. Those cases are not in the export.

Words every card uses

Decision support tool
The AI tool being measured. It gives clinicians advice, alerts, summaries or answers.
Output
One answer, alert or summary the tool gave for one request.
Go-live
The day the tool started being used on real patients. Before it, the developer tests the tool on cases outside the export.
Contact
One dated entry on an Epic AI session. The request and answer tables are kept by contact, so they match on CONTACT_DATE_REAL.
