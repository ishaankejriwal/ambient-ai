Can Epic's EHI export score the ambient AI metrics?

The framework spreadsheet lists 20 metrics for ambient AI scribes. This page writes each one as a formula in plain words, then shows which Epic EHI export table and column supplies each piece of it. Pieces Epic does not have are marked in red, with where to get them.

Of the 20 metrics, . Partly means Epic has some of the inputs, such as the notes to review or the patient groups, and a vendor, a survey or a reviewer supplies the rest.

The same 20 cards exist for Oracle Health and athenahealth. The formula and the variables are the same on all three pages, and only the tables and columns change.

Table, row and column

Table

The box around a variable shows where it stands. Visits is in the export. Note time is partly there: the export has a stand-in or part of the answer, and the rest comes from somewhere else. Survey score is not in the export.

The status beside each metric's name follows from those boxes. Yes means every variable is in the export. Partly means some are, or the export has a stand-in. No means none are. Some cards end the In Epic part with "Also in the export". Those are columns that help but sit outside the formula, and they do not change the status.

Every rate and percent change comes out as a fraction. 0.05 is 5 percent, or 5 percentage points when it is the gap between two rates. Count each case once. If the bottom of a fraction is zero, report N/A.

How to read the Epic site

The Epic EHI table index lists the export schema. Each table page describes its primary key and columns. Run calculations on your organization's exported files after checking its Epic version and local configuration.

  • Columns ending in _C_NAME are pick-lists. The page lists the allowed values, and sites can add their own.
  • Most provider, payer, procedure and diagnosis IDs come out as names. You get VISIT_PROV_ID_PROV_NAME, not VISIT_PROV_ID. Patient, visit, note, account and user IDs are real IDs.
  • DATETIME (UTC) and DATETIME (Local) are real timestamps. For plain DATETIME, check the column description before assuming it contains a time of day.
  • Tables join on PAT_ENC_CSN_ID (visit), NOTE_ID (note), PAT_ID (patient), USER_ID (the person logged in) and TX_ID (a charge or payment).

The four ambient tables

  • PAT_ENC_AMBIENT_SESSIONS: one row per ambient-session entry on a visit. The key is visit ID plus line number. Deduplicate AMBIENT_SESSION_IDENT when counting sessions. Confirm the vendor uses the same identifier before joining its logs. The audio is not exported.
  • NOTE_AMBIENT_SECTIONS: one row per ambient-section entry on a note. It includes the source session ID. Use it to link notes to sessions after validating local coverage. Its update marker does not establish whether a clinician changed the generated wording.
  • HOLOGRAM_AMBIENT_DX_INFO: one row per hologram contact, with the initial diagnosis name. Check whether a contact represents one suggestion in your workflow before counting it as one.
  • ENC_DX_ASSOC_AMBIENT_DX: one row per offered diagnosis the clinician kept as a visit diagnosis.

Everything else comes from the normal note, visit, patient and billing tables.

Words every card uses

Scribe visit
A visit with at least one row in PAT_ENC_AMBIENT_SESSIONS.
Scribe note
A note of the type the scribe drafts, usually the progress note, on a scribe visit.
Before weeks, After weeks
Two fixed periods, one before and one after a clinician started using the scribe. Confirm each start date against the rollout record.
Clinician
Epic exports provider names without their IDs, and two providers can share a name. Group results by a clinician identifier you have verified.
Comparison group
For any before-and-after metric, also compare against clinicians in the same specialty who did not get the scribe. Visit counts and timing also move with templates, vacations and panel changes.
